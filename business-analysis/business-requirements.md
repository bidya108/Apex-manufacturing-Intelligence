# Business Requirements

## Project

Apex Manufacturing Intelligence & Predictive Operations Platform

---

## 1. Management & Business Intelligence

### MBI-001
The system shall provide management with an overview of overall manufacturing performance.

### MBI-002
The system shall provide key performance indicators (KPIs) for production, downtime, quality and operational efficiency.

### MBI-003
The system shall allow authorized users to analyze KPIs by facility, production line, machine and time period.

### MBI-004
The system shall highlight significant operational issues requiring management attention.

---

## 2. Production Operations

### PROD-001
The system shall track production output across facilities and production lines.

### PROD-002
The system shall record production targets and actual output.

### PROD-003
The system shall calculate production efficiency.

### PROD-004
The system shall track production downtime.

### PROD-005
The system shall track production defects and quality metrics.

### PROD-006
The system shall allow production performance to be analyzed over different time periods.

---

## 3. Machine & Maintenance Management

### MNT-001
The system shall maintain information about manufacturing machines and their operating status.

### MNT-002
The system shall store historical machine sensor readings.

### MNT-003
The system shall maintain machine maintenance history.

### MNT-004
The system shall identify abnormal machine operating conditions.

### MNT-005
The system shall identify machines with elevated failure risk.

### MNT-006
The system shall provide maintenance priority information for machines requiring attention.

### MNT-007
The system shall allow users to view the historical condition and maintenance activity of a machine.

---

## 4. Data Management

### DATA-001
The system shall collect data from multiple manufacturing data sources.

### DATA-002
The system shall validate incoming data before it is used for analytical purposes.

### DATA-003
The system shall identify missing, duplicate and invalid data.

### DATA-004
The system shall maintain data quality metrics.

### DATA-005
The system shall maintain an audit trail for important data-related activities.

### DATA-006
The system shall maintain metadata describing important datasets and fields.

### DATA-007
The system shall restrict access to sensitive data based on user roles and permissions.

---

## 5. Analytics

### ANL-001
The system shall provide historical manufacturing data for analytical purposes.

### ANL-002
The system shall provide SQL-based analytical capabilities.

### ANL-003
The system shall calculate business and operational KPIs.

### ANL-004
The system shall allow users to analyze production, machine, maintenance and quality data.

### ANL-005
The system shall provide trend analysis over time.

### ANL-006
The system shall allow users to compare performance across facilities, production lines and machines.

---

## 6. Machine Learning

### ML-001
The system shall support machine failure prediction using historical operational data.

### ML-002
The system shall support predictive maintenance analysis.

### ML-003
The system shall identify anomalous machine behavior.

### ML-004
The system shall support production demand forecasting.

### ML-005
The system shall store model predictions and relevant prediction metadata.

### ML-006
The system shall provide model performance metrics for supported machine learning models.

---

## 7. API & Application

### API-001
The system shall provide REST APIs for authorized applications and users.

### API-002
The APIs shall provide access to approved manufacturing data.

### API-003
The APIs shall provide access to analytical results.

### API-004
The APIs shall provide access to machine learning predictions.

### API-005
The APIs shall support appropriate error handling and validation.

### API-006
The APIs shall provide machine-readable documentation.

---

## 8. Security

### SEC-001
The system shall require authentication for protected functionality.

### SEC-002
The system shall implement role-based authorization.

### SEC-003
The system shall restrict users from accessing functionality outside their assigned permissions.

### SEC-004
The system shall record important authentication and authorization events.

---

## 9. Reporting & Alerts

### REP-001
The system shall provide automated operational reports.

### REP-002
The system shall generate alerts for significant machine conditions.

### REP-003
The system shall generate alerts for high-risk machine failure predictions.

### REP-004
The system shall provide alerts for significant data-quality issues.

### REP-005
The system shall allow authorized users to view historical reports and alerts.

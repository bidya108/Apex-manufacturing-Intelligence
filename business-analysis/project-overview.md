# Apex Manufacturing Intelligence & Predictive Operations Platform

## Company

Apex Manufacturing is a fictional manufacturing company operating
multiple production facilities.

The company uses industrial machines and production lines to
manufacture products across different facilities.

## Business Problem

Apex Manufacturing generates large amounts of operational,
production, maintenance and quality data.

However, this data is distributed across different systems and
is not being effectively used for operational decision-making.

Management has limited visibility into machine performance,
production efficiency, downtime, product quality and future
operational risks.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to build an end-to-end
Manufacturing Intelligence and Predictive Operations Platform.

The platform will collect, manage, validate, analyze and
visualize manufacturing data while using machine learning
to generate predictive insights.

## Key Business Questions

1. Which machines are performing poorly?
2. Which machines are likely to fail?
3. What factors contribute to machine failures?
4. Which production lines have the highest downtime?
5. Which facilities are most efficient?
6. What factors are affecting production efficiency?
7. Which production batches have unusually high defect rates?
8. Can future production demand be forecast?
9. How much downtime is affecting production?
10. Which machines should be prioritized for maintenance?
11. Are there unusual patterns in machine sensor data?
12. What operational actions can be recommended from the data?
